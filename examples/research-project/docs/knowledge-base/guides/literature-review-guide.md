# Literature Review Guide

## Purpose
This guide provides a systematic approach to conducting literature reviews for research projects.

## Step 1: Define Research Questions
- Clearly state your research objectives
- Identify key concepts and terms
- Define scope and boundaries

## Step 2: Search Strategy
- Identify relevant databases (ACM, IEEE, arXiv, Google Scholar)
- Develop search queries using Boolean operators
- Track search terms and results

## Step 3: Paper Selection
- Apply inclusion/exclusion criteria
- Screen titles and abstracts
- Read full papers for relevant ones
- Document reasons for exclusion

## Step 4: Data Extraction
- Create standardized extraction form
- Record key information:
  - Authors, year, venue
  - Research questions
  - Methodology
  - Key findings
  - Limitations

## Step 5: Synthesis
- Identify themes and patterns
- Compare and contrast approaches
- Note gaps in literature
- Build conceptual framework

## Step 6: Documentation
- Maintain bibliography
- Create concept documents for key ideas
- Document findings in research notes
- Cross-reference related work
