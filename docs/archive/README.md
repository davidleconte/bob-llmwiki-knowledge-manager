# docs/archive/

Historical records preserved verbatim. These files are **not authoritative** — they
reflect what was believed at their creation date and contain metrics that have since
been retracted or superseded. See [STATUS.md](../../STATUS.md) for the canonical
maturity status and [docs/README.md](../README.md) for the live documentation index.

## Contents

| Category | Files |

Savings figures cited in these files (`68.96%`, `89.3%`, `91.80%`) are fabricated and
withdrawn; see [`evaluation/validation-disclaimer.md`](../../evaluation/validation-disclaimer.md)
for the retraction record.

## Banner status — stated accurately

Most files here carry a retraction/deprecation banner. **Three do not:**
`COMPARISON.md`, `design-document-kb-addendum.md` and `mece-framework.md`. Until
2026-07-25 this page asserted that *all* of them did, which was false for seven files
and is the kind of blanket claim that lets a reader trust the directory's
self-labelling more than it deserves. Treat every file here as historical whether or
not it says so at the top.

## What moved out

The nine-chapter book series that used to live here was **rewritten as a live artifact**
on 2026-07-25 and now lives at [`docs/book/`](../book/table-of-contents.md) — 12 chapters
and 3 appendices, in the savings/metric/link gates' scope. It was moved rather than left
here because an archived document that reads as current is worse than either a
maintained one or a clearly dead one.
