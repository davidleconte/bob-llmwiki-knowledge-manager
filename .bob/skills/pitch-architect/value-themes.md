# Reusable Value Themes

Capability-level angles to draw on when helping find a value wedge. Never present these as product pitches — always as capability + value, in "you" language.

## Hybrid by design (deployment flexibility)

The value is that the buyer keeps control of *where their software, data, and workloads live* — on-premises, public cloud, private cloud, or any mix — and can move between them without re-platforming.

- **Unconsidered need it unlocks:** freedom from lock-in, and the ability to meet data-residency, latency, and regulatory needs without standing up parallel stacks.
- **Contrast with:** cloud-only approaches that force one location.
- **Spoken version:** "It runs wherever your data lives — any cloud, your data center, or both — and you can change your mind later without starting over."

## Consolidate vs. connect & govern (data value)

Two complementary moves for making enterprise data usable, best drawn side by side:

- **Consolidate (the lakehouse):** data that should live together moves — once — into one open home. Open formats mean every engine reads the same single copy; the cheapest engine wins each job. Retires downstream extracts and duplicate copies rather than adding one.
- **Connect & govern (the fabric):** data that can't or shouldn't move stays put. A catalog knows what exists and where; rules travel to the data. Govern once, use everywhere.
- **Spoken contrast:** "Move in what should live together; reach out to what must stay put. Together it all acts like one open, governed place."

## Adding themes

Add new themes the user surfaces during intake, always phrased as capability + value, never as a named product. Record: the value in one sentence, the unconsidered need it unlocks, what it contrasts with, and a spoken version.
